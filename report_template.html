Schedule luck report

Playoff odds

How often each team makes the playoffs if the same weekly scores are replayed against thousands of random schedules with the league's real format.

Schedule luck

Expected win % is the average record across all simulated schedules. Teams above the dashed line won more than their scores earned (lucky); teams below won fewer (unlucky). Color shows the luck index: actual minus expected win %, in percentage points.

What if you had their schedule?

Each row is a team; each column is whose real schedule it plays. A cell is that team's record against that schedule. The diagonal is each team's actual record.

Where each team finishes

Share of simulations ending at each seed. Seeds left of the dashed line make the playoffs.
